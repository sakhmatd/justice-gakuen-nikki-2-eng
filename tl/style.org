How each recurring character talks in English. The examples come from
finished translations. Names are in [[file:glossary.org][glossary.org]].
Taken over from the first game on 2026-11-17; its examples come from that
game's text. Add characters new in this game as they are settled.

* General
- Write like a native English game localizer. Keep the meaning, then
  choose what this speaker would say in English. Do not keep Japanese
  sentence order or set phrases word for word.
- Students talk casually: contractions, "gonna", "ya", "huh". Teachers and
  Hyo talk like adults.
- Sound effects and shouts stay short, in English onomatopoeia.
- Literal vs natural:
  | JP                   | literal                     | natural                   |
  |----------------------+-----------------------------+---------------------------|
  | 今年もよろしくな     | Please treat me well again  | Here's to another year!   |
  | 気がすまない         | It doesn't settle my mind   | I can't let it go         |
  | 頑張ります!          | I will persevere!           | I'll give it my all!      |
  | どうしたの?          | What happened?              | What's up?                |
- Item messages (を手に入れた) start with "Got", not "You got".

* Speech markers
| JP marker               | speaker, usually      | EN voice                        |
|-------------------------+-----------------------+---------------------------------|
| ワシ, じゃ, のう, おぬし | Gan                   | folksy: "ain't", "y'see", "oughta" |
| オイラ                  | Shoma                 | boyish, cocky, "ya"             |
| だぜ, ちまう, 根性       | Batsu, Hayato         | brash, loud                     |
| ネ, ヨ, ユー (katakana) | Roy, Tiffany, Boman   | simple, plain English, no accent spelling |
| わよ, かしら, のよ       | girls, Kyoko          | natural, not prissy             |
| 私 + だ (male, formal)  | Hyo                   | cold, clipped, formal           |

* Characters
Insert ids: each speaker has a fixed id in the name inserts ({v3:0} is
Batsu's form of the protagonist's name, {v6:1} Hinata's). {v3} is the
bare name, {v4}/{v5} add -kun, {v7} -san. {v6} adds -kun to a surname
only, {v8} adds -san to a surname and -chan to a given name. Keep the tag
the JP uses. A character's own lines are nearly always the same JP in the
male (g0) and female (g1) events, and one EN serves both (see
Protagonist).
- Batsu: hot-blooded, impulsive, big-hearted. Short shouted lines.
  His mother is Shizuku; Principal Raizo is his father. Hates dogs and
  is always hungry.
  俺/オレ, お前, だぜ, ねぇ, やがって, くそぅ, よぉし. Says {v3:0} and お前
  to both protagonists; his lines do not change by route. Treats the
  protagonist as his best buddy (いいコンビ, "a great team"). Delivers
  evening papers part-time and wants to do right by his mother. Clueless
  in a sincere way (takes a pinwheel for a ninja tool). EN: "Yo",
  "gonna", "Man", "!!". 根性 is "guts", 母さん "Mom", 親父 "old man".
  First game: "Full poooower......!!" (全か～～い).
  - "My bike chain snapped on me! Man, what luck..."
  - "So we're even! That's us, a great team!!"
- Hinata: cheerful, tomboyish, teases Batsu.
  Learned martial arts from a correspondence course and spars with
  Sakura. Loves cakes.
  私. Male route {v6:1}, female {v8:1}. Calls Natsu 夏っちゃん
  ("Natsu-chan"), Batsu, Kyosuke and the Chairperson by name. Bubbly and
  quick to pout: もう!/んもう = "Geez!", えへへ "Hehe", ふっふっふ, stretched
  words (だ～い好き, "lo~ve"). Her katakana tails (アリガト, ネ, ナ) only
  add cuteness; write plain "Thanks". Her style is the Ken Masters
  correspondence course. A sweet tooth (oversweet honey lemons). On the
  male route she has a crush and hides it with excuses; the same lines
  read as close friendship on the female route.
  - "Geez, {v6:1}! You think it's a joke 'cause it's by mail, huh? Mine
    is the time-honored Ken Masters fighting style!"
  - "Heh heh heh, beat me with a wish like that? A hundred years too
    early!"
- Kyosuke: calm, polite, dry. Discipline committee.
  Hyo's twin brother. Wears glasses; likes chess and shogi.
  僕, 君/キミ, plain だ/な/さ/ね. Male route {v4:2} (-kun), female {v7:2}
  (-san). Lives alone, cooks when he has time and takes vitamin pills
  when he does not. Proud and private: he rejects pity, and thanks
  people quietly with a pause ("...Thank you"). Fond of the protagonist
  but never effusive. EN: complete, measured sentences, light
  contractions, no slang.
  - "{v4:2}. I don't regret the way I live one bit. Spare me the pity,
    would you?"
  - "...Not bad, {v4:2}. This turned out pretty well."
- Shoma: Gorin baseball ace. Cocky, competitive, friendly.
  オイラ, じゃん, ぜ, だろ, へへ～ん. Says {v3:3} on both routes and once
  mocks the girl with {v3:3}{col:0}さん{col:-}. アニキ/兄貴 (Shuichi) is
  "Bro", 修兄ちゃん "my bro Shuichi", 流兄ちゃん "big bro Nagare"; calls
  Natsu 夏. Rival-buddy who turns everything into a bet. A benchwarmer
  this summer who dreams of Koshien. Female-route lines add "girl or
  not, no holding back" (女だからって手加減しねぇぞ). EN drops g's
  ("hittin'", "headin'"), "ya", "y'know".
  - "I'm Shoma Sawamura. Nice to meet ya!"
  - "Heh heh, I win!! And it was a homer, so two juices!! Sorry, but a
    bet's a bet!"
  - "Next year I'm hittin' a homer and takin' us to Koshien!"
- Natsu: Gorin volleyball. Brisk and sporty.
  アタシ, アンタ, わ/わね/かしら, ふ～ん, ふふっ. Male route {v6:4}, female
  {v7:4} (-san). Calls Hinata ひな ("Hina") and Shoma 将馬. Big-sisterly
  and teasing, sure of herself, a good cook. On the male route she makes
  him a bento and flirts (hearts, 女のカン "woman's intuition"); the female
  route keeps the same lines as friendship. EN: casual and dry, never
  prissy.
  - "Hmm... Your taste buds are kind of a kid's, huh..."
  - "Hehe, woman's intuition. ...Actually, I watched what you ate in the
    cafeteria afterwards."
- Roberto: Gorin soccer. Friendly and earnest.
  俺, お前, だぜ, な, さ, スマン ("Sorry"), サンキュー. Male route {v3:5},
  female {v7:5} (-san). Aims for the world stage and trains alone after
  club. Shouts 爆裂! ("Explosive!") with his moves; 爆裂Vゴール is Victory
  Goal. Well-meaning but tactless ("you can take a hit"). Treats the
  protagonist as a training partner. EN: straightforward, sporty.
  - "Sorry, {v3:5}. Good thing it was you, though. You're built tougher
    than the rest."
  - "You practice when nobody else does, and you practice when they do.
    That's what hard work is."
- Gan: Gedo. Huge, slow, folksy old-man speech.
  ワシ, おぬし, じゃ, のう, ぞい, わい, じゃい, ガッハッハ ("Gahaha!"). Male
  route {v3:11}; the female route adds {col:0}さん{col:-}, which stays
  "-san" in colour. With the girl he is shy and gallant
  (おぬしほどの女はそうはおらん). Always hungry: rice balls, whole
  watermelon. EN: "ain't", "ya", "reckon", "a mite", g-dropping.
  - "Gan Isurugi. Edge, ain't you gonna give your real name?"
  - "This oughta be good. Let's see how far the new guy can go."
  - "Well, y'see, I couldn't accept the answer to a science problem, so I
    was gonna try an experiment."
  - "Gahaha, look who it is, {v3:11}! This'll be a cinch!"
- Edge: Gedo delinquent. Sneering, mocking.
  オレ/俺, オマエ/おめえ/てめえ, ぜ, ねえ, でやんの, ダチ ("pal"). Says
  {v3:9} on both routes. エッジ様 is "the great Edge", ヒャハハハ "Hyah hyah
  hyah!". Vain about his hair and sends people on errands, but wants
  honesty from friends and gets angry at flattery. On the male route an
  answer of 好き makes him back off in a panic (MGT09); keep it a light
  gag. EN: "ain't", "ya", "Tch", g-dropping.
  - "Hyah hyah! Akira's chewing you out again."
  - "Don't just nod along to butter me up! We're pals, I wanted the
    truth!"
- Akira: Gedo. Terse. Rides a motorbike.
  Daigo's younger sister. In the helmet (アキラ) she passes as a boy: 俺,
  clipped and commanding. Bare-faced (あきら) she is gentle and timid: 私,
  わ. Works as a bike mechanic.
  Helmeted she says {v3:10} and お前, だな/だろう, スマン, and calls Daigo
  兄貴 ("Bro"). Bare-faced she says {v5:10} (male route, -kun) or
  {v8:10} (female), ね/よ, and 兄さん ("Big brother"). Under stress the
  helmet voice slips into 私 (MGT0A); keep the slip in EN ("I... I'd
  never..."). Strict about rules out of care for others.
  - "Of course. Why would I take it off?" (helmet)
  - "Sorry, everyone. Roller coasters scare me, so I couldn't ride
    without my helmet." (bare-faced)
  - "No. Better to run. You can still make it." (helmet)
- Hyo: student council president. Cold and formal.
  Heads the Justice High council. Kyosuke's twin brother. Uses 私.
  Also お前, 我々, stern old-fashioned forms (ねばならん, 見事...みよ, 笑止!,
  輩, 立ち去れ), フ.../フン. Says bare {v0} on both routes ({v0}よ is a
  formal call). Carries a sword and talks about a "super elite" for
  Japan. The male protagonist says 雹さん with です/ます; the female says
  雹クン and talks casually. EN: no contractions, slightly archaic.
  - "Absurd! Japan began to decay the moment it gave itself to greed!
    Do you not see that!?"
  - "I have no use for those who decide their own limits! Begone!"
- Roy, Tiffany, Boman: Pacific. Their katakana Japanese becomes simple,
  correct English.
  - "Ahaha, Japanese is so hard. By the way, what's your name?"
- Roy: ボク, キミ, ぜ, かい, ワット!/ホワット! ("What!?"). Says {v3:6} on
  both routes. Rich (a limousine), cocky and quick to sulk, with a
  sensitive nose. Likes the protagonist on both routes and competes with
  Tiffany for their time. EN: casual American, "OK!", no accent.
  - "OK! Let's take a little drive in my limo on the way home!"
  - "The amusement park? You're more of a kid than I thought, {v3:6}.
    ...But I kinda like that about you."
- Tiffany: ミー, ユー, ネ/デース/ヨ/まショー, English words (GOOD IDEA,
  ソーリー, サンキュ, Wait!), hearts. Says {v3:7} on both routes. Bubbly and
  clingy; her father is a company chairman. Her slips (デンキ for テンキ)
  stay as a cute mistake in EN: "such lovely electricity today!"
  (MGG04P), first game "were you weather?". Otherwise plain, correct
  English with "Hiii!" and stretched vowels.
  - "Sorry, {v3:7}. My papa's clothes are king size. Japan doesn't sell
    many."
  - "Ooh! I see! GOOD IDEA! Let's go buy it!!"
- Boman: 私, あなた, always です/ます. Says {v4:8} (-kun) on both routes.
  Devout Christian (God, Mass, the chapel), gentle, against killing even
  a frog. Very attached to the protagonist. EN: formal, mostly without
  contractions ("let us", "Is something the matter?").
  - "Then I will come to your house for you on Sunday, {v4:8}. I thank
    God that I may go to Mass with you..."
  - "What!? You do not understand at all which matters more, a precious
    life or a class!"
- Hayato-sensei: hot-blooded PE teacher, macho and loud.
  俺, お前, ねえ, ぜ, かってんだ, おうし!, ヨーイ!ドン! ("Ready... go!"). Says
  {v3:16} on both routes. Takes on any contest and turns up everywhere.
  Teases the protagonist about Kyoko-sensei. 青春 is "youth" (first
  game: "This is what they call youth", "Youth, you big idioooot!"). On
  the female route: "Girl or not, ya gotta train!" EN: "ya", g-dropping.
  - "What's with that pathetic showing? Life is long, but youth is short!"
  - "Don't underestimate me! I'm a PE teacher! If I can't take a
    student's tackle, how could I take a student's love!?"
- Hideo-sensei: gentlemanly, calm.
  私, 君, です/ます, なさい. Says {v0}{col:0}君{col:-} ("-kun") on both
  routes. Strict about study but forgives an honest answer, praises with
  エライ! ("Well done!") and gives out Shimazu-Style books. Sentimental
  and a little lonely; worries about the protagonist's love life
  (彼女/彼氏 by route). His comic admiration (男同士なのにほれぼれする)
  becomes 年の差なんか関係なく on the female route. EN: polite, warm.
  - "A game center! You meant to go there instead of studying?"
  - "Well done! Well done, {v0}{col:0}-kun{col:-}!"
- Kyoko-sensei: school nurse. Warm, gently teasing.
  Sloppy off duty; likes sake and horse racing. Her Rival Schools
  ending has her accept Hideo-sensei's proposal.
  私, あなた, わ/わよ, ちょうだい, かしら. Male route
  {v1}{col:0}クン{col:-} ("-kun"), female {v1}{col:0}さん{col:-} ("-san");
  her lines are otherwise the same. Flirts with students and tests odd
  treatments on them; チュッ is "Smooch" (keep the JP heart tag). Has a
  wistful side (her first love by the river). EN: playful adult.
  - "Yes, take it all off, please."
  - "Good, I'm glad. You really helped me figure it out. Thanks.
    Smooch"
- Raizo: principal. Pompous and long-winded.
  Batsu's father, Imawano by name.
  わし, 君, かね, たまえ, よかろう, うむ, ハッハッハ. Says
  {v0}{col:0}君{col:-}. Students call him 学園長 ("Principal", "sir").
  Teaches 忌野流 ("Imawano-Style") training and charms, and lets slip fond
  remarks about Shizuku. EN: grand old man, "Hm", "Very well", "Ha ha
  ha!".
  - "Ha ha ha, you're a serious one. Very well, I shall teach you one
    Imawano-Style technique."
  - "Who knows. At least Shizuku... ahem! Well, it's only a charm. No harm
    in trying."
- Chairperson (委員長, iinchou): Taiyo class chairperson, Hinata's friend,
  the game's guide in the 4/1 tutorial. Always "Chairperson", never
  "class rep". Calm, responsible, organised; a little bossy and dryly
  teasing when she explains. Brushes off questions about herself. Natural
  girl's speech (わよ, わね), never prissy. Keep game terms in her
  explanations exact (button names, menu names in {col:0}).
  - "You came here without even knowing that? Fine, I'll explain."
  - "Me? I'm the Chairperson. Just call me Chairperson."
- Protagonist: plain casual voice. The female protagonist is a little
  softer, never prissy.
  Male events are g0, female g1, the same scene. Male: 俺 (僕 or 自分 to
  teachers), お前 to friends, だろ, だな, さ, よぉ. Female: 私, uses names
  where he says お前, わ/わね/の, でしょ, かしら, あら, ハァイ, ましょ,
  ごめんなさい. Her katakana tails (ネ, ヨ, ナ) are playful; write plain
  EN. Seniors: he says 流さん, 修一さん, 雹さん; she says 流先輩, 修一先輩,
  雹クン ("Hyo-kun", casual). Lines in （） are inner thoughts: write them
  in ASCII parentheses, without the {sp} indents. Unbracketed monologue
  stays plain narration. Other characters' lines are usually the same JP
  on both routes, so their one EN must fit a boy and a girl. Avoid "man",
  "dude", "guy", "bro" or he/she for the protagonist unless the JP says
  男/女.
- Daigo-senpai: Daigo Kazama, Gedo's top boss (総番長) and Akira's older
  brother. A third-year about to graduate. Gruff and honourable; lectures
  on being a real man (漢) after watching from the side. 俺, お前, だぜ.
  Says {v4:17} ("-kun") on both routes. Turns up at the end of a scene
  to judge what the protagonist did, then leaves (あばよ, "Later"). His
  lessons are about real kindness (思いやり, 男の優しさ). EN: gruff, short.
  - "Sometimes even a little kid has to pull through on his own. But
    when he really can't, you lend a hand and say nothing."
  - "Getting hung up on titles like top boss is no way for a real man
    to act!"
  - "If you think things alone can win someone's heart, you're dead
    wrong."

** New in this game
- Ran (Ran Hibiki): Taiyo first-year who wants to be a journalist. She
  is joining the newspaper club, whose entrance test is a scoop photo.
  Hunts scoops and ambushes people for interviews, Hyo included. Pushy,
  quick to flare up and quick to forgive; flustered when teased about
  love. Spirited, never prissy. ワタシ/私, calls the protagonist アンタ,
  わよ, じゃない, stretched vowels (ど～いう).
  - "Hee hee, don't worry about it! Oh, I forgot to introduce myself.
    I'm Ran. Future journalist. Nice to meet ya!"
  - "Not bad! You talked the council president around. Thanks to you,
    I got my interview."
- Nagare (Namikawa): Gorin third-year, swim team ace, Shuichi's friend.
  Shoma grew up with him and calls him 流兄ちゃん ("big bro Nagare").
  On land he is spaced out: very few words, lines open with "..." and
  break off mid-sentence, 僕. In the water or in a crisis he turns sharp
  and decisive, 俺. Kind and modest; thanks embarrass him.
  - "...I wasn't... trying to scare him..."
  - "...I see. Good. Nobody who loves swimming is a bad person..."
  - "...Stop it... You're embarrassing me..."
- Shuichi-kun: Shuichi Sawamura, Shoma's older brother. Gorin
  third-year, the baseball club's former ace, sidelined by an injury; he
  runs the baseball club. Easygoing, dependable big brother. 俺, な.
  - "Hey, if it isn't you! What's got you in such a rush?"
  - "He's a different guy in the water. Normally he's off in the
    clouds, but the second he's in, he's all business."
- Kotaro (Okita-sensei): volleyball club adviser, a former member of
  the All-Japan national team. Hot-blooded but upbeat, less macho than
  Hayato-sensei. 僕 with お前. Pet lines: "Let's aim for the world
  together!" and グーッだ ("Great stuff!").
  - "Here to join volleyball? I'm the adviser, Kotaro Okita! I'm gonna
    work you hard this year!"
- Mari: Mari Nanase, first-year soccer club manager. Attentive, gentle,
  polite to everyone, classmates included: 私, です/ます, Roberto-kun.
  - "Oh, there you are. I've been waiting. Welcome to the soccer club!"
  - "Too bad about the quota. Maybe my cheering wasn't good enough?"
- Kanayama (Sado-sensei): Gengoro Sado, outside coach of the sumo club,
  not a teacher. 金山 was his ring name as a wrestler. Students call him
  監督 ("Coach") or Sado-sensei. Gruff, warm old pro, demanding but
  fair: わし, お前, ひよっこ ("greenhorn"). Plain gruff English, not
  Gan's folksy dialect.
  - "Welcome, greenhorn. Picked sumo, eh? You've got guts. Good, good.
    I'll drill you hard all year."
  - "Then hit him head-on! Remember a whole year of training!"
- Kozue-senpai: Kozue Yamabuki, second-year art club vice-president
  (she does not look it). Breezy, easygoing senior, responsible but
  tired of explaining things. わよ, ね, "OK", "はーい"; calls the
  protagonist "-kun" (クン).
  - "Hi there, welcome! You're the new one, right? I'm the
    vice-president, Kozue Yamabuki. New members are always welcome!"
  - "OK, that's it for the explanation. Explaining stuff wears me out."
- Shizune: Shizune Kujo, first-year who teaches new members in the brass
  band. Quiet, expressionless, very polite: わたくし, です/ます, many
  pauses ("..."). Stutters and freezes when nervous; faints from shock.
  - "...Oh... hello. Are you here to join? I'm Shizune Kujo. I'll be
    teaching the new members... Nice to meet you."
  - "...R-really? I'm... nervous, you know. It just doesn't show..."
- Sayuri-senpai: Sayuri Tsukishima, third-year drama club president, a
  star of male roles with fan clubs at nearby schools. Theatrical,
  confident and warm. ワタシ, キミ ("you"); mixes です/ます with わ.
  - "Do you like theater? I'm the drama club president, Sayuri
    Tsukishima. Nice to meet you."
  - "I know better than anyone how hard you've worked this year."
- Hakase: Hyoe Kase, chemistry club adviser. Insists on "Hakase" (a pun
  on Kase), no honorific, never "sensei". Chummy, eccentric professor
  who tests dubious potions on students and shrugs off the results. 僕,
  たまえ, かね, ふむふむ, よしよし, なるほどなるほど.
  - "Don't call me sensei. Call me Hakase. I'm the chemistry club
    adviser. Glad to know you."
  - "Would I ever give a student something like that? Relax, relax."
  - "...Oh dear, another failure..."
- Sakura: Sakura Kasugano, the Street Fighter guest. サクラ in the name
  table, さくら in dialogue. Cheerful, sporty, mad about street fights;
  spars with Hinata. わたし, casual, "へへ"; calls the protagonist by
  nickname.
  - "Heh heh, I came to invite you! You're free over spring break,
    right?"
  - "Isn't it obvious? Street fighting!"
