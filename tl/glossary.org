- ハカセ is "Hakase" (user, 2026-11-17), kept romanized like the
  honorifics. He is the chemistry club's teacher, 加瀬 兵衛 (Kase Hyoe,
  given name probably read ひょうえ; MGG04G "担当は加瀬 兵衛先生"), who
  says "don't call me sensei, call me Hakase" (a pun on his name). Write
  "Hakase" with no honorific. 加瀬先生 stays "Kase-sensei".
- Full names: 月嶋 小百合 Sayuri Tsukishima, 山吹 こずえ Kozue Yamabuki,
  九条 静音 Shizune Kujo, 七瀬 まり Mari Nanase, 沖田 光太郎 Kotaro Okita
  (沖田先生 Okita-sensei), 佐渡 源五郎 Gengoro Sado, 加瀬 兵衛 Hyoe Kase,
  沢村 修一 Shuichi Sawamura. Seniors take -senpai from a first-year
  (流先輩 Nagare-senpai); the male MC also says Nagare-san/Shuichi-san
  where the JP has さん.
- 金山 is the sumo ring name (四股名) of the outside sumo coach 佐渡 源五郎
  (Sado Gengoro), called 監督 or 佐渡先生. Speaker label and ring name:
  "Kanayama". 佐渡先生 is "Sado-sensei", 監督 "Coach". 佐渡 金山先生 is
  "Sado Kanayama-sensei" (the Chairperson mixes the two).
- あきら vs アキラ (checked 2026-11-17, against the user's first guess):
  アキラ is the helmeted, boyish persona, and あきら is the bare-faced
  girl. Evidence: MGK10 g6 s0070, MGM02 g0 s0431 (Daigo: the helmeted
  アキラ is a made-up personality), MGG04O g0 s0472-0490. Both are "Akira"
  in EN. Helmeted Akira speaks curtly and formally; bare-faced Akira uses
  soft girl's speech (style.org). The text does not mark the persona by
  spelling: アキラ (~700 uses) covers both, also in narration after the
  helmet comes off, and あきら occurs only in Daigo's MGM02 scene and the
  MGK10 g6 s0070 quiz. Write plain "Akira" with no colour; the portrait
  and the voice show the persona. Where a line contrasts the two, say it
  in words ("helmeted Akira", "Akira without her helmet").
